	Bounds $l \le f \le r$, any costs, no source/sink. $s \to t$ flow of exactly $F$: add $t \to s$ with $[F, F]$, cost 0. Supplies $b_v$: extra vertex $X$, edges $X \to v$ $[b_v, b_v]$ ($b_v > 0$), $v \to X$ $[-b_v, -b_v]$ ($b_v < 0$). Infeasible $\Rightarrow$ \texttt{solve().first = false}.
]
