\entry{Flow models}
\textbf{Bounds} $[l, r]$: cap $r - l$, $d_v = \sum_{\text{in}} l - \sum_{\text{out}} l$; $S \to v$ cap $d_v$ ($d_v > 0$), $v \to T$ cap $-d_v$ ($d_v < 0$). Feasible iff maxflow$(S,T) = \sum_{d_v > 0} d_v$; flow $l + f$. With $s, t$: add $t \to s$ cap $\infty$, then max $=$ maxflow$(s,t)$ on the residual (keep $t \to s$); min $= f_{t \to s} -$ maxflow$(t,s)$ without $t \to s$. Min cost: \S1.1.3.
\textbf{Vertex cap} $c_v$: $v_{\text{in}} \to v_{\text{out}}$ cap $c_v$.
\textbf{Closure}: $S \to v$ cap $p_v > 0$, $v \to T$ cap $-p_v$; $u$ needs $v$: $u \to v$ cap $\infty$. Max $= \sum_{p_v > 0} p_v -$ mincut, $S$ side.
\textbf{Bipartite}, max matching $M$: min vertex cover $= M$ (K\"onig; $(L \setminus Z) \cup (R \cap Z)$, $Z = S$ side); max indep.\ set $= |V| - M$. Hall: $L$ matchable iff $|N(X)| \ge |X|$ $\forall X \subseteq L$.
\textbf{DAG}: min disjoint path cover $= n - M$ (edges $u_{\text{out}} v_{\text{in}}$); on transitive closure: min chain cover $=$ max antichain (Dilworth).
